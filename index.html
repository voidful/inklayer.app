InkLayer

繁體中文

InkLayer is a note-taking app that puts handwriting, PDFs and Markdown notes on one canvas, in files you own.

What it does

  • Write on PDFs with Apple Pencil or a stylus. Your handwriting is kept beside the PDF; the original file is never changed.
  • Markdown notes with links between notes, backlinks and a graph of how they connect.
  • Layers: the PDF, typed text, handwriting and images stay separate, so each can be shown, hidden or exported on its own.
  • Canvases for pages, cards, recordings and flowcharts side by side.
  • Your files, synced your way: a library is an ordinary folder of Markdown and PDF files. Keep it in iCloud Drive, sync it through your own Google Drive, or use a WebDAV server you choose. InkLayer has no account and no server of its own.
